An Italian table, twenty minutes from the reef

La Tavola Italiana Limited brings the pantry of Italy to Kenya’s South Coast: DOCG wines from Piedmont and Tuscany, DOP cheese and cured meats, cold-pressed olive oils and bronze-die pasta, sourced directly from small and mid-size Italian producers. We supply households in Diani and Ukunda, hotels and lodges along the coast, and the community of residents and visitors from around the world who miss the taste of home or are discovering it for the first time.

  • Rooted on Galu Beach RoadA physical shop and cold store in Diani, stocked and refrigerated for wine, cheese and salumi.

  • Retail and wholesaleSingle bottles for a beach picnic, or standing wholesale orders for restaurants and hotel kitchens.

Ordering takes three steps

No app to download. Everything runs through WhatsApp, phone or a visit to the shop, any hour of the day.

Message us

Send your order on WhatsApp or call us directly. Our assistant can recommend wines, oils and pairings if you’re not sure what to pick.

Confirm & pay

We confirm stock and price, then you pay by M-Pesa, card, or cash on collection. You’ll get an order summary either way.

Collect or get it delivered

Pick up in-store on Galu Beach Road, or have your order delivered across Diani and the South Coast.

Good to know

What are your opening hours?

We’re open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Visit the shop or message us on WhatsApp at any time.

Do you deliver, and where?

Yes, delivery is available across the South Coast (Diani, Ukunda, Galu, Tiwi), and we can arrange courier delivery to Mombasa and further afield in Kenya for wholesale orders.

Can I order in bulk for a restaurant or hotel?

Absolutely. Wholesale pricing is available for restaurants, hotels and lodges along the coast. Message us on WhatsApp with your product list and quantities for a quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa, major debit and credit cards, and cash in-store. International guests can also pay by card on collection or delivery.

Are your products authentic Italian imports?

Yes, our wines, oils, cheeses and cured meats are imported directly from producers in Italy, many carrying DOP, DOCG or IGP protected designations.
