OUR STORY

An Italian table, twenty minutes from the reef

La Tavola Italiana Limited brings the pantry of Italy to Kenya’s South Coast: DOCG wines from Piedmont and Tuscany, DOP cheese and cured meats, cold-pressed olive oils and bronze-die pasta, sourced directly from small and mid-size Italian producers. We supply households in Diani and Ukunda, hotels and lodges along the coast, and the growing community of residents and visitors who miss the taste of home — or are discovering it for the first time.

  • Rooted on Galu Beach Road

    A physical shop and cold store in Diani, stocked and refrigerated for wine, cheese and salumi.

  • Built for WhatsApp ordering

    Message our assistant, get product recommendations and pairings, confirm your order and pay — all from your phone.

  • Retail and wholesale

    Single bottles for a beach picnic, or standing wholesale orders for restaurants and hotel kitchens.

FAQ

Good to know

Do you deliver outside Diani?

Yes. We deliver across the South Coast (Diani, Ukunda, Galu, Tiwi) same-day, and can arrange courier delivery to Mombasa and further afield in Kenya for wholesale orders.

Can I order in bulk for a restaurant or hotel?

Absolutely, wholesale pricing is available for restaurants, hotels and lodges along the coast. Message us on WhatsApp with your product list and quantities for a quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa, major debit and credit cards, and cash in-store. International guests can also pay by card on collection or delivery.

Are your products authentic Italian imports?

Yes, our wines, oils, cheeses and cured meats are imported directly from producers in Italy, many carrying DOP, DOCG or IGP protected designations.

How do I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp at +254 794 433309, tell our assistant what you're after, and we'll confirm availability, price and delivery time before you pay.
